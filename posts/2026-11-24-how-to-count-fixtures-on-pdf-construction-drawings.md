# How to count fixtures on PDF construction drawings

Counting outlets, lights and fixtures by hand on a PDF drawing set is slow and easy to get wrong.

## A faster way

Click each symbol on the plan and keep a running count per item, so the takeoff is finished when you reach the last sheet.

[ContractorCounter](https://www.contractorcounter.com) does this for construction drawings.
