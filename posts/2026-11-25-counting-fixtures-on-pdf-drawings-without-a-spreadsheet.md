# Counting fixtures on PDF drawings without a spreadsheet

![Counting fixtures on PDF drawings without a spreadsheet](https://different-otter-416.convex.cloud/api/storage/6600e507-4f60-49dd-9162-c5c74d5dd9f7)

Counting outlets, lights and fixtures by hand on a PDF drawing set is slow and **easy to get wrong**.

- Click each symbol on the plan
- Keep a running count per item
- Finish the takeoff when you reach the last sheet

> The count lives with the drawing, so the whole team sees the same number.

ContractorCounter is construction markup and takeoff software that runs in the browser. More at [ContractorCounter](https://www.contractorcounter.com).
